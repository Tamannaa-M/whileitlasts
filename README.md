While It Lasts

**While It Lasts** is a cozy focus timer where every study session becomes a small visual ritual. Choose a piece of ice, light a candle, or catch a mystery flight, then stay focused until the journey ends.

🌐 **Live website:** [tamannaa-m.github.io/Study-app-Unique-timers-](https://tamannaa-m.github.io/Study-app-Unique-timers-/)

## How it works

Choose one of three focus experiences:

- **Ice:** Pick an ice shape and study while it slowly melts into a puddle.
- **Candle:** Light a candle and focus while the flame burns and the wax melts.
- **Catch a Flight:** Choose a boarding pass, take off from Mumbai, and focus until the flight lands at your destination.

The session duration can stay hidden, allowing you to focus without watching the clock. If you prefer, you can reveal the timer at any time.

## Features

- Hidden randomized focus durations
- Separate tracking for real study time and object progress
- Five distinct ice and candle tiers
- Animated melting ice, growing puddles, candle wax, and flames
- Sun, heater, hot water, fan, and wind interactions
- Mystery destination boarding passes
- Original takeoff and landing videos
- Sunny, rainy, sunset, and moonlit flight views
- Changing flight conditions, altitude, journey progress, and ETA
- Cozy café, bedroom, forest, library, and rooftop spaces
- Movable to-do, notes, YouTube, vinyl, and settings widgets
- Built-in ambience and volume controls
- Session history and productivity statistics
- Local browser storage for notes, tasks, settings, and history
- Responsive layout for desktop, tablet, and mobile

## Privacy

While It Lasts does not require an account or send study data to a server. Notes, tasks, settings, and session history are stored locally in each visitor's browser.

## Run locally

Download the project and open `index.html` in a browser. Keep the `assets` and `resource` folders beside `index.html` so the flight artwork, videos, and ambience can load.

For the short testing mode, add `?debug=1` to the page address.

## Deploy with GitHub Pages

1. Upload `index.html`, `favicon.svg`, `README.md`, and the complete `assets` and `resource` folders to the repository root.
2. Open the repository's **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, select **Deploy from a branch**.
4. Select the `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder.
5. Save and wait a few minutes for GitHub Pages to publish the website.
